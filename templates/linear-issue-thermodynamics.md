# Thermodynamics reviewer

## Goal

Capture eQuilibrator feasibility summaries with assumptions and uncertainty.

## Artifact Contract

- Update only compact ledgers, docs, templates, schemas, or runtime pointers.
- Do not install tools, call paid providers, upload data, mirror databases, or store raw biological artifacts.
- Keep outputs as planning, prioritization, reference context, target evidence, control evidence, or execution proof.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Claim boundary is explicit and conservative.
- Required provider, tool, license, upload, and data-policy gates are named before any future live execution.
- The lane cannot satisfy biological validation or target-host production claims by itself.
