# Campaign Model

A BioProspector campaign records a research question, target, host, and
supporting evidence. A manifest links the records for routes, reaction steps,
and candidates. Reviewers use those records to compare hypotheses and decide
what to investigate next.

## What a Campaign Records

- Route alternatives and unresolved steps
- Candidate evidence, conflicts, and reasons for rejection
- Search coverage and source references
- Route comparisons by evidence, host fit, and remaining gaps
- Claims linked to the evidence that supports them

Use the [first campaign guide](FIRST_CAMPAIGN.md) to build the manifest,
the [ledger reference](dossier-schema.md) to interpret the records, and the
[workflow guide](WORKFLOWS.md) to choose the next review step.
