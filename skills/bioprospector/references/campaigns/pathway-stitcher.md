# Pathway Stitcher Campaign

Status: draft v0

Use this campaign after route and enzyme candidate shortlists exist.

## Goal

Assemble candidate enzymes into coherent routes and score each integrated design hypothesis.

## Required Outputs

- `route-stitching-scorecard.tsv`
- `host-fit-scorecard.tsv`
- `candidate-ranking-ledger.tsv`
- `pareto-frontier-ledger.tsv`
- `bottleneck-map.md`
- `minimal-designs.md`
- `red-team-report.md`

## Stitching Checks

- intermediate compatibility
- stereochemistry
- cofactors
- host precursor availability
- toxicity
- transport
- compartment
- product protection
- side reactions
- missing validation evidence

## Route rankings

Always report:

- minimal-gene route
- highest-evidence route
- clearest validation handoff route
- best host-fit route
- ambitious de novo route
- diversity-library route
