# <Campaign>: Evidence Handoff

## Agent Role

Validation-readiness planner.

## Scientific Goal

Convert route and candidate uncertainty into non-procedural evidence gaps,
measurement categories, and control categories.

## Artifact Contract

- Update `assay-handoff-ledger.tsv`.
- Name measurable products, readout categories, control categories, and risks at planning level.
- Update `claim-ledger.md` for unresolved validation gaps.

## Claim Boundary

Do not write wet-lab protocols, construct designs, concentrations, timings, or
procedural steps.
